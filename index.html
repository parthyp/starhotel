Explore the Area

The Grand Canyon, Bearizona & the Mother Road

Williams sits at the crossroads of northern Arizona's best adventures. Here's what's close to Star Hotel.

About 1 hour drive

Grand Canyon National Park

Head north on AZ-64 to the South Rim for sunrise at Mather Point, the Rim Trail and the Desert View Watchtower — then come back to Williams for dinner on Route 66.

~5 min walk

Grand Canyon Railway

Ride the historic train from the Williams depot straight to the South Rim. Leave your car parked free at the hotel.

Minutes away

Bearizona Wildlife Park

Drive through forest habitats with black bears, wolves and bison, then walk the Fort Bearizona exhibits.

Right outside

Historic Route 66

Neon signs, vintage diners and souvenir shops line Williams' famously preserved stretch of the Mother Road.

Nearby

Williams Golf & Outdoors

Play the Elephant Rocks golf course, hike Bill Williams Mountain or fish the area's pine-ringed lakes.

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Call us at (928) 635-1515.

How far is Star Hotel from the Grand Canyon?

Williams is the closest town on Interstate 40 to the Grand Canyon. The South Rim of Grand Canyon National Park is about an hour's drive north on AZ-64, and the Grand Canyon Railway departs from downtown Williams, about a 5-minute walk from the hotel.

Can I walk to the Grand Canyon Railway?

Yes. The Grand Canyon Railway depot in downtown Williams is about a 5-minute walk from Star Hotel, so you can leave your car parked for free at the hotel.

Is parking and Wi-Fi free?

Yes. Parking and high-speed Wi-Fi are complimentary for all guests.

What is included in the rooms?

Every room includes free Wi-Fi, a coffee maker, a microwave, a mini fridge, a flat-screen TV, a work desk and a private bathroom.

Is Star Hotel close to Bearizona Wildlife Park?

Yes. Bearizona Wildlife Park is just a few minutes' drive east of downtown Williams along Route 66.

How do I book a room?

Book directly on our website through our secure reservation system, or call the hotel at (928) 635-1515. Reservations are made directly with the hotel; please review the hotel policies when booking.
